# Musiki Lugatı

> Turkish makam music, the modal art music tradition of the Ottoman and Turkish lands.

Resources for researching, reading, notating and computationally studying Turkish makam music, including Ottoman-era sources and Turkish folk music. Curated by [Musiki Lugatı](https://musikilugati.com).

## Reference Works

- [Kubbealtı Lugatı](https://lugatim.com) - Turkish dictionary with music terminology and literary usage examples.
- [TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr) - Scholarly encyclopedia with signed entries on makams, forms, instruments and musicians.

## Notation and Repertoire

- [DîvânMakam](https://divanmakam.com) - Community platform with a score archive in Turkish notation and an encyclopedia.
- [İSAM Arşivi](https://arsiv.isam.org.tr) - Archival collections of the Center for Islamic Studies, including the Cüneyd Kosal Turkish music collection.
- [Nota Mecmuası](https://www.notamecmuasi.com) - Online score archive searchable by makam, usul, form and composer.
- [Repertükül](https://www.repertukul.com) - Turkish folk music encyclopedia with scores, lyrics, biographies and regional information.

## Manuscripts and Critical Editions

- [Corpus Musicae Ottomanicae](https://www.uni-muenster.de/CMO-Edition/en/cmo/cmo.html) - Critical editions and an online source catalogue of 19th-century Ottoman music manuscripts in Hampartsum and European notation.

## Datasets

- [OTMM Makam Recognition Dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/58408) - Pitch tracks, metadata and makam/tonic annotations for 1,000 recordings in 20 makams.
- [SymbTr](https://github.com/MTG/SymbTr) - Machine-readable scores of 2,200 works in 155 makams, available as text, MusicXML, MIDI and PDF.

## Research Platforms

- [Dunya Makam](https://dunya.compmusic.upf.edu/makam) - Web application linking scores, recordings and metadata with audio-score alignment. Requires registration.

## Software

- [Mus2](https://mus2.com.tr/en/) - Notation software for microtonal music with built-in Turkish makam accidentals and tunings.
